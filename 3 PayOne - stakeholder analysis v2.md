**## 1. INTRODUCTION**



\# 1.1 Purpose of stakeholder identification and analysis



Stakeholder identification and analysis is a fundamental component of the planning and requirements-analysis

stage of the PayOne database project. The objective is to identify all individuals, groups, organisations and

institutions that either:



* Participate directly in PayOne's business operations.
* Provide services, infrastructure or resources necessary for its operations.
* Generate and supply data to PayOne as well as receive data from PayOne.
* Use information generated by PayOne to make operational, managerial or strategic decisions.
* Influence the organisation's regulatory, technological, commercial or operational environment.



PayOne's database must accommodate multiple stakeholder categories, different transaction and transaction relationships, interconnected business processes and several levels of information access. Taking into account its varied business processes and services, this stakeholder analysis informs the following activities:



* Business process identification and modelling.
* Database scope and boundaries.
* Business rules and data requirements.
* Entity identification and relationships.
* Transaction and information flows.
* Data ownership and accountability.
* User roles and access privileges.
* OLTP and OLAP database requirements.
* Security, privacy and regulatory requirements.
* System integration requirements.

**## 2 PAYONE STAKEHOLDER IDENTIFICATION**



Stakeholders of PayOne may be categorized into the following broad groups:



* Internal stakeholders: Staff of PayOne who own, manage, operate or use the database.
* Primary external stakeholders: Individuals, entities and organisations that directly purchase, use or participate in PayOne's products and services.
* Institutional and business partners: Organisations that provide financial, technological, telecommunications and other supporting services.
* Regulatory and oversight stakeholders: Institutions responsible for regulating, supervising or monitoring aspects of PayOne's operations.
